Research statement

I study how assistive robotic systems can translate human intent into useful action in everyday environments. My work combines accessible interaction design with perception, motion planning, and embedded control. At Cornell’s Architectural Robotics Lab, I develop Home+, a gesture-controlled robotic furnishing research prototype for people with upper-limb mobility limitations. My broader research interests include spatial behavior and the evolution of interactive systems through long-term design research.

ACADEMIC BACKGROUND

Research experience

At Cornell’s Architectural Robotics Lab, my contributions to Home+ span robot modeling, gesture-to-action integration, perception, motion planning, and embedded feedback. My advisors are Keith Evan Green, Tapomayukh Bhattacharjee, and Haym Hirsh.

Previously, at the Design and Augmented Intelligence Lab, advised by Saleh Kalantari, I developed data collection and visualization tools for indoor wayfinding research and supported user studies.
