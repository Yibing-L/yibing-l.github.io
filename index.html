Research statement

I study how assistive robotic systems can translate human intent into useful action in everyday environments. My work combines accessible interaction design with perception, motion planning, and embedded control. At Cornell’s Architectural Robotics Lab, I develop HomePlus, a gesture-controlled robotic furnishing research prototype for people with upper-limb mobility limitations. I also examine the program’s seventeen-year design history to understand how an assistive-robotics inquiry continues across changing artifacts and research conditions.
