Architectural Robotics Lab, Cornell University

Research Assistant

Advisors: Keith Evan Green, Tapomayukh Bhattacharjee, Haym Hirsh

home+ — Gesture-Controlled Assistive Robotics

Research contributions
  • Led development of home+, a gesture-controlled mobile assistive robot for people with upper-limb mobility limitations.
  • Integrated gesture recognition and RGB-D perception to translate arm and hand movements into robot actions.
  • Developed ROS 2/MoveIt 2 motion planning and encoder-based closed-loop control for coordinated base-and-arm manipulation.
  • Prepared study protocols and experimental materials and obtained IRB approval to evaluate gesture interaction under upper-limb movement constraints.
  • Led a retrospective of home+’s design evolution (2009–2026), resulting in a first-author manuscript under review at HRI 2027.
